# FindMyDentist

**A dentistry-first platform connecting patients, dentists, clinics and dental students.**

FindMyDentist (FMD) brings AI-guided dental help, dentist discovery, booking, records and clinic operations together under one identity.

## What we're building

| Workspace | For | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **FMD** | Patients | AI dental assistant, find and book dentists, records vault, community |
| **FMD Clinic** | Dentists and clinic staff | Calendar, patients, treatment plans, billing, messages, analytics |
| **FMD Academic** | BDS / MDS students and faculty | AI seminar studio, research, journal club, cases, viva prep |

## Stack

Next.js · React · TypeScript · Tailwind CSS
